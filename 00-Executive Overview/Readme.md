
# ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin  
## Executive Overview

---

## Overview

ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin is a Product Management decision intelligence framework that focuses on how product decisions are made when real-world execution does not go as planned.

Unlike traditional product documentation that assumes stable execution, this project models how Product Managers respond when:

- Roadmaps shift unexpectedly
- Assumptions fail
- Data becomes incomplete or unreliable
- Stakeholder priorities change
- Delivery constraints impact execution

---

## Problem Statement

In real product environments, execution rarely follows the plan.

Product Managers often face:

- Changing business priorities
- Unclear or evolving requirements
- Delivery delays and resource constraints
- Unexpected risks and dependencies
- Conflicting stakeholder expectations

However, most PM documentation does not capture **how decisions are actually made under these conditions**.

---

## Solution

This framework introduces a structured approach to decision-making under uncertainty through:

- Decision Logs
- Assumption Tracking
- Trade-off Analysis
- Scenario-Based Thinking
- Crisis Response Frameworks

It ensures product decisions remain transparent, traceable, and aligned with business outcomes even when execution is unstable.

---

## Core Focus Areas

### 1. Decision Intelligence
Capturing what decisions are made, why they are made, and their impact.

### 2. Assumption Management
Identifying and tracking assumptions behind product strategy and delivery.

### 3. Trade-off Management
Balancing scope, time, quality, and resources under constraints.

### 4. Scenario Thinking
Preparing for “what-if” situations when plans break.

### 5. Adaptive Governance
Ensuring product direction adjusts dynamically to real-world conditions.

---

## Key Outcomes

- Improved decision transparency
- Better prioritization under constraints
- Stronger stakeholder alignment during change
- Reduced ambiguity in execution decisions
- Structured handling of uncertainty in product delivery
