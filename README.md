# ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin

## Overview

This repository explores the **decision-making side of Product Management when execution does not go as planned**.

While most product documentation focuses on structured delivery, this project focuses on what happens in reality:

> When assumptions fail, timelines shift, data is incomplete, priorities change, and crises emerge — how does a Product Manager make decisions?

---
## Purpose

To demonstrate structured Product Management decision-making in environments where plans evolve, risks emerge, and execution requires continuous adaptation.

This repository is designed to show **how Product Managers think, decide, and adapt under uncertainty**, not just how they plan execution.

---

## 🔥 What Makes This Repository Different

Most PM portfolios focus on:

- PRDs  
- Roadmaps

- Delivery planning  

This repository focuses on:

> **Decision intelligence under uncertainty**
