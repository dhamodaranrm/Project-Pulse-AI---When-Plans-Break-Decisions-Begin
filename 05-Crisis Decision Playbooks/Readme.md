
# Crisis Decision Playbooks Documentation

## Overview

This folder contains the crisis decision management framework used within **ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin**.

The purpose of these documents is to provide structured guidance for making decisions during high-impact events, delivery disruptions, operational failures, stakeholder escalations, or unexpected business challenges.

Crisis Decision Playbooks help Product Managers respond quickly, maintain alignment, and minimize business impact when normal plans are no longer sufficient.

---

## Why Crisis Decision Playbooks are Needed

Even with strong planning, risks, assumptions, and scenario preparation, unexpected events can still occur.

Examples include:

- Major delivery failures
- Critical milestone delays
- AI prediction failures
- Data quality incidents
- Stakeholder escalations
- Resource shortages
- Significant roadmap changes

Crisis Playbooks provide predefined actions and decision paths to help teams respond effectively under pressure.

---

## Documents Included

### Crisis Decision Framework

Defines the principles, decision process, escalation paths, and governance approach used during crisis situations.

### Crisis Playbook Register

Captures common crisis scenarios, triggers, response actions, decision owners, and recovery approaches.

### Crisis Review & Lessons Log

Evaluates crisis responses, captures lessons learned, and identifies improvements for future incidents.
