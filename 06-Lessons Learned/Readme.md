
# Lessons Learned Documentation

## Overview

This folder captures key lessons, insights, and improvement opportunities identified throughout the lifecycle of **ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin**.

The purpose of these documents is to ensure that decisions, assumptions, trade-offs, scenarios, and crisis responses are continuously reviewed so future initiatives can benefit from past experiences.

Lessons Learned help transform experience into organizational knowledge and support continuous improvement.

---

## Why Lessons Learned Matter

Successful Product Management is not only about making decisions but also about learning from outcomes.

Lessons Learned help teams:

- Understand what worked well
- Identify what did not work as expected
- Improve future decision-making
- Reduce repeated mistakes
- Strengthen delivery and governance practices

---

## Documents Included

### Continuous Improvement Plan
