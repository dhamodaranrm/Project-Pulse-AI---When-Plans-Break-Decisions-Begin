
# Assumptions Register Documentation

## Overview

This folder contains the assumption management framework used within **ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin**.

The purpose of these documents is to identify, track, validate, and monitor assumptions that influence product strategy, delivery planning, stakeholder expectations, and business outcomes.

---

## Documents Included

### Assumptions Register

Captures key business, product, delivery, and operational assumptions that impact decision-making.

### Assumption Validation Log

Tracks whether assumptions remain valid, require revision, or have been proven incorrect over time.

### Assumption Risk Assessment

Evaluates the potential impact and risk associated with failed or changing assumptions.

---

## Key Deliverables

- Assumption Tracking Framework
- Assumption Validation Process
- Assumption Risk Analysis
- Decision Support Inputs

---

## Key Outcomes

- Improved decision quality
- Early identification of hidden risks
- Better planning under uncertainty
- Increased transparency in decision-making
- Reduced impact of incorrect assumptions
