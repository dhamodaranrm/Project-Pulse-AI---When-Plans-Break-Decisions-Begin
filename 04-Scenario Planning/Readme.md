
# Scenario Planning Documentation

## Overview

This folder contains the Scenario Planning framework used within **ProjectPulse AI – When Plans Break & Decisions Begin**.

The purpose of this section is to prepare for multiple possible future outcomes by identifying risks, uncertainties, and alternative execution paths before they impact delivery or product success.

Scenario Planning helps Product Managers and stakeholders make better decisions when conditions change, assumptions fail, or priorities shift.

---

## Why Scenario Planning is Needed

In real product environments, plans rarely follow a single predictable path. Teams must deal with:

- Changing business priorities  
- Unexpected delivery delays  
- Data or AI model failures  
- Resource constraints  
- External dependencies  
- Shifting stakeholder expectations  

Scenario Planning ensures the team is prepared for multiple possibilities instead of relying on a single “ideal” plan.

---

## Documents Included

### Scenario Framework

Defines how scenarios are structured, evaluated, and categorized based on risk, impact, and probability.

### Scenario Analysis Register

Captures key scenarios, triggers, impact assessment, and recommended response strategies.

### Scenario Response Plan

Defines actions, mitigation strategies, and decision paths for high-impact scenarios.
